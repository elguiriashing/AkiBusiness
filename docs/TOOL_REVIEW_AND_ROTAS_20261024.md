# AkiBusiness tool review and rota implementation

Reviewed 24 October 2026 against `main` at `190be83`. This is a source-based product and workflow review, supplemented by automated tests and first-party competitor documentation. It is not a claim that every live customer journey, integration, assistive technology or hardware printer has been exercised with a signed-in customer account.

## Catalogue and product boundaries

The paid toolbox has twelve authoritative keys in `akibusiness_private.catalogue()` and `assets/toolbox.js`: CRM, Inbox, Tasks, Calendar, Inventory, PoS, Sales, Marketing, Automation, Team Chat, People and Knowledge. Additional navigation destinations are not additional paid toolbox slots. Rotas is a new destination under the existing People entitlement in this release. Pricing, 4/8/12 allowances and customers' saved tool selections are unchanged. A later decision can make scheduling an independently selectable thirteenth tool, but that requires coordinated billing and entitlement changes.

Customers should remain on AkiBusiness throughout their operational work. AkiHQ is the free company/listing hub; it must not become a repeated redirect hop. This change adds no cross-domain onboarding hops. Customer acquisition and subscription-route changes already in other work remain outside this branch.

## Implemented in this change

- Collapsible EN/ES workflow instructions for all twelve tools; create-record shortcuts where an existing supported form is available.
- Clear labels for planning-only Marketing and Automation behavior and the distinction between operational sales records and fiscal issuance.
- Business Marketing shows workspace contacts rather than platform acquisition segments. It explicitly says these are not a consent list. People shows workspace contacts instead of the platform user count in ordinary business workspaces.
- Keyboard activation for existing elements with button semantics, and keyboard focusability for task cards. Knowledge's icon-only delete action receives a name.
- A separate `#/rotas` view and a direct link from People and Calendar help. New assets are included in source and paid-release bundles and offline asset versions are updated together.

These changes improve the starting journey across twelve tools. They do not implement all proposed competitor gaps.

## Rotas: customer workflows and implemented scope

**Manager:** AkiBusiness → Rotas → add from Teams or create manual profile → add shifts/repeating pattern → review warnings → save draft → publish → print/export. Team and manual profiles share the same shift editor. A linked Gmail/Google account is just an existing team account: no Gmail mailbox is opened or imported.

**Linked staff:** open the same workspace → Rotas → see own last-published shifts → print or export. The account needs People permission under the current entitlement model. Staff cannot see other staff, manual profiles, managers' absence reasons, unpublished edits, the full staff directory or draft templates through the rota RPC.

**Unlinked staff:** manager creates a name/department/hours target → assigns shifts → prints a personal copy or exports the selected person's calendar. No invitation, login or workspace membership is created.

Implemented options:

- Staff names, departments and per-person weekly-hours targets; reuse active workspace members, or create/edit/remove manual profiles.
- One or more shifts per day, split shifts, explicit overnight end date, up to 24 hours per shift, unpaid breaks, role/job, area/location and notes.
- Repeat until a chosen date with configurable 1–28 days on, 0–28 days off and weekday selection; handles month/year boundaries and leap days. Repeat horizon is bounded to a year.
- Named reusable shift templates; copy a visible date period forward; edit/delete individual shifts.
- Manager-recorded time off/unavailability with private reasons. Availability/absence overlaps and shift overlaps block publication in the database. Weekly-hours targets generate warnings, not an assertion of legal compliance.
- 1/2/4-week, month and custom date windows; per-person filter; list and grid views; 12/24-hour display; IANA timezone selection. Grid displays at most the first 31 days with a notice; list/export retains the chosen full period.
- Separate draft and published documents; explicit confirmation for publishing the **complete saved rota**, independent of current filters. Confirmation explains this. No messages are sent automatically.
- A4 or Letter, portrait or landscape, whole-team or a page per staff member; selected individual copies; browser print-to-PDF; CSV and ICS exports with explicit saved-draft/published choice.
- Scheduled hours use local wall-clock duration minus breaks, including DST transition dates. This is explicitly planning, not actual elapsed payroll or attendance data. ICS uses the configured timezone.
- Up to 200 staff, 1,000 shifts, 1,000 absence records and 100 templates in one location's current rota document. Limits fail visibly rather than silently truncating records.
- Revision-checked writes serialize concurrent changes, including the first save. Save/publish actions retain actor/revision metadata. Rota business data stays in the database and is not written to localStorage.

Next scheduling work: staff-requested leave approval, recurring partial-day availability, shift swaps/claimable open shifts, acknowledgement and delivery notifications, shift coverage requirements/qualifications, configurable rest rules, archive/history viewer, payroll/actual attendance integration and independent scheduling-tool billing if approved. Those capabilities are not represented as shipped here.

## Accessibility and usability acceptance

New rota inputs have visible labels, native buttons and date/time controls, keyboard-accessible scroll regions, explicit table headings, status/error messages and visible focus. Primary controls target 44px height; layouts allow horizontal scrolling inside the schedule, not the whole page. Shift creation/editing requires no drag gesture. Print status is textual rather than color-only, and reduced-motion is respected. Date/time interpretation remains explicit.

[W3C dragging guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements.html) requires a pointer alternative to drag-only functionality. [Target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) describes a 24px minimum with exceptions; 44px is this implementation's touch target, not a claim that WCAG AA mandates 44px everywhere. A full screen-reader/contrast conformance audit of the entire legacy app remains outstanding.

Client-success acceptance is outcome based: a manager can produce a saved, conflict-free, printable rota without changing domains; a staff member can find only their own published shifts; a manual staff profile can receive a paper copy without an account. For other tools, use the final column of the comparison table as the next acceptance scenario. Suggested usability targets (not measured results): first rota in under ten minutes, repeat a normal week in under two minutes, no repeated account creation or unexplained cross-product navigation.

## Verification and release

- `tests/rotas.test.mjs`: duration, recurrence, invalid input, weekly warnings, CSV/print/ICS safety, twelve bilingual guides, late response isolation and publish guard.
- `tests/rotas-db.test.mjs`: real PostgreSQL engine (PGlite) executes the migration; tests staff privacy, membership/entitlement denial, manual profile behavior, first/stale saves, server validation, overnight conflicts, draft/publish separation and audit metadata.
- `tests/rotas-browser.cjs`: isolated frontend acceptance with simulated RPCs, EN/ES × phone/tablet/desktop × light/dark. It verifies Teams/manual profiles, overnight repetition, publication, editing without altering the published copy, in-memory form preservation, grid and exports. It does not use customer data or impersonate a live customer.
- Existing gateway, billing, inventory, checkout, tenancy and production packaging tests must pass before merging. A new CI workflow runs rota database and browser acceptance.
- Additive database migration: `20261024020749_staff_rotas.sql`. It uses private-schema tables with RLS enabled and no browser table privileges; checked private RPC implementations and public invoker wrappers. Existing shared authorization helpers enforce People access and administrator writes.
- Apply the migration before releasing the frontend; verify unauthorized calls deny access. No billing migration or customer-data backfill is required. No existing member roles, prices or tool selections are changed.
