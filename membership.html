A CLEAR MONTHLY RHYTHM

Available now. Ready for next month.

01

Active toolbox

These are the tools your location can use now. Saving a different selection does not activate it early.

02

Saved next toolbox

Your saved selection starts at the next eligible monthly rotation. A replacement removes one active tool and adds another; repeated saves do not increase your allowance.

03

Your team, without the wait

Grant or revoke staff access to your active tools whenever you need. Permissions stay scoped to the location and never unlock an unlicensed tool.

Annual subscribers also rotate monthly. Billing frequency does not lock a toolbox for a year. Access changes after a confirmed upgrade follow the purchased package; downgrades take effect on the applicable billing date. Existing tool data is retained.
